The right information at specification stage can help you make more informed product decisions.
Sustainability requirements shouldn’t become a question you’re trying to answer after the specification has already been written.
Bringing them into the conversation earlier gives you more opportunity to consider the right products, systems and supporting information from the outset.
And that’s where we can help. Our team can work with architects and specifiers at an early stage to understand the requirements of your project, explore suitable product options and identify the technical and environmental documentation available to support your specification.
How we can support you:
  Product options considered around your project
We’ll take the time to understand the application and wider specification requirements before helping you explore suitable solutions.
  Technical and environmental documentation
We can help identify the relevant product information available, including EPDs and certification where applicable, so you have supporting documentation at specification stage.
  Technical expertise when you need it
Our team is here to help you navigate product and system requirements and provide practical support throughout the specification process.
  A considered approach to material choice
Through Pyro + Echo, we also work with manufacturers selected with consideration given to factors including quality, environmental standards and responsible production - offering further choice where design and responsible sourcing both matter.
The earlier we’re involved, the more opportunity there is to build these considerations into your specification from the outset.
So whether you’re reviewing individual products, developing a complete tiling specification or looking for the documentation to support your selections, talk to us early.
