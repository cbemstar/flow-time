From substrate preparation and waterproofing to fixing, finishing and surfaces, discover the brands behind The Trimline Group.
When you’re delivering a tiling project, there are a lot of different products, systems and requirements to bring together.
At The Trimline Group, we make that simpler.
Our specialist brands, alongside products from leading third-party manufacturers, give contractors, developers, architects and specifiers access to more of what they need from one trusted supply partner.
Here’s what sits behind our Group:
 
  Atrim
Profiles for the protection and finishing of surfaces and edges, including tile trims, movement joints and specialist finishing profiles.
 
  Dukkaboard
Tile backer boards, wetroom and substrate solutions designed to support a wide range of project requirements.
 
  OnBoard
Tile backer board solutions providing further choice across different applications and specifications.
 
  Validus
Preparation, fixing and finishing products including adhesives, levelling compounds, grouts, primers, screeds and silicones.
 
  Pyro + Echo
A considered collection of design-led tiles and surfaces for architects, designers and specifiers.
And that’s only part of the picture.
Alongside our own brands, we also supply products from leading third-party tiling manufacturers, helping you bring together substrates, waterproofing, adhesives, grouts, trims, tools, accessories and finished surfaces through one supplier.
Because every project is different, our team can also help tailor products and systems around your individual requirements —  backed by technical and specification support, fast delivery, personal service and competitive rates.
From the substrate behind the installation to the finished surface, we bring the complete package together.
